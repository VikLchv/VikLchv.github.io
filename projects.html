Data roles in 2026

How the data world changed

Every role has its own silhouette. Axes follow the skill map order: business at the top, data on the right, operations and AI at the bottom, science on the left. Click a role to compare it with me above.

Modelling got cheaper

Few people train their own models. Most use, tune and evaluate existing ones. Statistics and experiments stay, LLM evals joined them.

The AI Engineer arrived

Apps on top of LLMs: RAG, agents, evals, deployment. Barely a role in 2024, one of the most wanted now.

Analysts are not dashboards

AI writes the SQL and the basic charts. Value moved to the right question, the context and the recommendation.

Semantics is the new currency

For AI to answer correctly over data, someone has to define what the data means. Semantic layers turn analytics engineers into context engineers.

Business analysis is back

When AI speeds up the output, understanding the problem becomes the expensive part: requirements, processes, decisions.

The borders blurred

One person with AI can ship the pipeline, the model, the report and the app. Companies look for people who deliver a whole data product.
